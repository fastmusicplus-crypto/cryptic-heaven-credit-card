# Risk and compliance discovery

This checklist is for organizing expert review. It is not legal, regulatory, tax, investment, or security advice, and it does not determine that any activity is permitted.

## Legal and program structure

- Identify each legal entity, target market, customer type, and activity.
- Obtain qualified advice on lending, payments, stored value, custody, money movement, marketing, consumer protection, and digital-asset rules that may apply.
- Establish which licensed or regulated parties perform each activity and who owns customer obligations.
- Review required agreements, disclosures, complaints handling, records, and regulatory reporting with the responsible parties.

## Credit and customer outcomes

- Define the legal and economic nature of the product before designing collateral thresholds.
- Model rapid asset-price declines, liquidity gaps, concentrated collateral, market closures, and price-feed failure.
- Define eligibility, limits, repayment terms, notices, delinquency processes, hardship paths, and customer dispute processes.
- Explain whether collateral can be sold, when it can be sold, how sale proceeds are applied, and what happens if proceeds do not cover amounts owed.
- Test that customer-facing amounts and notices match the authoritative ledger and program agreements.

## Custody and asset safety

- Document who controls signing keys and withdrawal authority, including emergency access and dual control.
- Review segregation, reconciliation, counterparty concentration, insolvency treatment, recovery, and insurance claims carefully; do not imply protections that are not contractually and legally established.
- Consider supported networks, token contract risks, upgrade/admin powers, bridge exposure, reorganization/finality assumptions, and withdrawal congestion.
- Define incident handling for key compromise, chain outage, frozen assets, erroneous transfers, and partner unavailability.

## Card and operational risk

- Threat-model card issuance, credentials, authorization, retries, duplicate messages, refunds, chargebacks, fraud, and account takeover.
- Establish reconciliation between authorization, clearing, settlement, customer balances, and partner statements.
- Define liquidity monitoring, settlement cutoffs, shortfall escalation, and business continuity.
- Put change approval, access reviews, vendor oversight, penetration testing, incident response, and independent assurance on the plan.

## Privacy and data protection

- Inventory identity, financial, transaction, device, and support data and its purpose.
- Determine lawful collection, retention, access, deletion, localization, and customer rights with qualified advisors.
- Keep cardholder data and identity details out of public blockchain records and logs unless a reviewed necessity and lawful basis exist.
- Limit access to sensitive information; define breach notification ownership and incident contacts.

## Required gate before live customer or asset use

Do not use real funds or customer payment data until the accountable program parties have documented approval for legal structure, customer terms, controls, security, custody, reconciliation, support, incident response, and partner readiness.
